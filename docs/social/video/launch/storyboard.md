# Arcveil — "Live on Arc" launch film (storyboard v1, 2026-09-17)

Product-launch explainer in the grammar of the Arcus mainnet film
(`~/Downloads/xoR5YfstnUSXgr_3.mp4`, 37 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps): white studio
world, soft 3D glass objects, product typography that types in word by word,
a UI ledger that checks itself off, a token beat, an end card. Built entirely
on Higgsfield: plates from GPT Image 2.5 → Seedance 2.5, type and UI in
Higgsedit, narration from Seed Audio, assembly in the Higgsfield sandbox.

Palette is the brand's, not the reference's: white ground, navy `#1b3158`
type, mint-deep `#1f9d4c` for the accent word, mint `#85ed75` for glows,
particles and pills. Type: Manrope Semibold for headlines (the reference's
friendly geometric sans, in our own face), DM Mono for eyebrows, ledger rows,
pills and the URL, same as the banners and the header.

## Build plan (all on Higgsfield)

1. **Style frames** (this round): GPT Image 2.5, high, 2k, 16:9, with two
   reference frames passed as `image_references` and the instruction "match
   only the rendering style: white studio, soft glass, shallow depth of
   field; palette navy and mint, not blue". The Band mark PNG is passed as a
   reference for P3, P5 and P6 so the geometry is ours.
2. **Plates**: Seedance 2.5 omni-reference, 1080p, 24 fps, `generate_audio`
   off, one plate per shot at the shot's length plus 1 s of handle. Motion
   direction per plate is in the shots table; camera never cuts, only drifts.
3. **Type and UI**: Higgsedit project 1920×1080 at 30 fps. Fonts added with
   `fonts add` (Manrope Semibold, DM Mono Regular/Medium from the repo).
   Word-by-word reveal via token text with the accent word last; ledger rows
   slide up 12 px and fade, checks pop with a spring; pills scale from 0.9.
4. **Narration**: Seed Audio, Grady, one take per shot so each line can be
   placed at its beat; `speech_rate` adjusted per line.
5. **Assembly**: one `sandbox_exec` builds the project, places plates, type
   and VO, renders H.264 1080p, verifies duration, and PUTs to the reserved
   `media_upload` URL; `media_confirm` afterwards. Master copied to
   `docs/social/video/launch/arcveil-live-on-arc.mp4`.

## Higgsedit edit (`higgsedit/edit.jsx`)

Single build script: VO on the spine, every plate a composed `media` node with
a 0.45 s fade from white, type in Manrope 700 / DM Mono via `fonts add`,
headlines as rows of text nodes so the accent word keeps its colour, word-by-word
`text.motion`, ledger rows and checks staggered with frame choreography, the
Band mark drawn as a cubic-bezier path (Higgsedit paths have no arcs).
`HF_FRAMES=t1,t2 higgsedit build edit.jsx` renders check frames; `HF_RENDER=1`
renders the movie at 12 Mbps.

## Master (rendered 2026-09-17)

`arcveil-live-on-arc.mp4`: 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 12 Mbps + AAC, 46.28 s,
rendered by Higgsedit in the Higgsfield sandbox (7 render windows), uploaded as
Higgsfield media `c0709f74-1bf2-4ea2-a88d-56d05faf904a`. The build script that
produced it is `higgsedit/edit.jsx` (rev 5). Higgsedit lessons baked into it:
`row`/`column` take no choreography and no frames, and stack animated text
vertically, so every multi-segment headline is a `frame layout=row` with text
widths pre-measured in Pillow (the `TW` table); text inside a `hug` frame needs
an explicit width; overshoot easing must not touch opacity. Decided by the user:
token beat kept, Manrope headlines, straight to master with no revision pass.
